# Humanize writing

Applies whenever a skill produces prose for the user or writes a `## Log`
entry — not a skill in its own right, referenced from AGENTS.md's voice
rules the way `RESOLVING.md` is referenced rather than invoked. Adapted
from [AshwinSathian/humanize-writing-skill](https://github.com/AshwinSathian/humanize-writing-skill).

Writing reads as AI-generated more from *shape* than word choice: uniform
sentence rhythm, safe generic claims instead of specific checkable ones,
templated structure. Fix the shape first.

## Write this way

- **Commit to specific, checkable claims.** Replace "plays a significant
  role" or "experts say" with the actual fact and its source. Unknown? Say
  it plainly, not dressed up as more authoritative.
- **Use the plain verb.** "Is," not "serves as/stands as/represents." One
  strong verb, not a padded phrase.
- **Vary sentence rhythm on purpose.** Don't let every sentence or
  paragraph land at the same length.
- **Default to commas or periods.** Save the dash for genuine emphasis or
  interruption, not filler between clauses a period handles just as well.
- **Reach for concrete, checkable detail over abstraction.**
- **Cut hedge-intensifiers**, not just banned nouns: rather, very, little,
  pretty.
- **Let structure follow the content, not a template.** Triadic lists,
  rhetorical questions, "Despite these challenges..." pivots: fine when
  earned, not as a default shape.
- **Never invent facts, numbers, or sources to sound more specific.**
  Specificity has to come from something true.

## Scope

- A Log entry is short-form (see AGENTS.md: read as current state, not a
  history) — specificity and plain verbs still apply, but there's no
  paragraph rhythm to vary.
- A card's formatting rules (headings, bullets, bold labels, one emoji per
  heading) are the exception, not padding — structural scanning aids stay.
- Never silently soften a user's own explicit wording, a decision's
  reverse-if condition, or a direct quote — humanize the skill's own
  prose, not what it's quoting or recording verbatim.

## Quick self-check before finalizing

1. Same length/shape every sentence or paragraph? Vary it.
2. A claim that could be specific without inventing anything? Make it
   specific.
3. Padded verb, stock transition, unearned rule-of-three? Cut it.
4. More than one or two dashes in a stretch? Convert most to commas or
   periods.
5. Tells present — delve, testament to, tapestry/landscape, moreover,
   "it's not X, it's Y," vague "experts say" attribution? Fix them.
