# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Gambit
- **Category**: Personal strategy tool. An AI thinking partner that keeps a written record of your goal.
- **Description**: A strategist in your pocket that helps you think a hard goal through, decide, and do the next thing.
- **Stage**: Built and working, pre-audience. Open source, free, deployed on Vercel.
- **Website**: N/A. Repo: github.com/skyf0xx/gambit

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Ambitious generalists of any technical level. These are people carrying one hard goal with no boss, board or coach to plan it with: growing or starting a business, leaving a career, moving a family, running a campaign.
- **Key Problem**: Hard goals stall in the gap between ambition and the next concrete move. People who plan with a general chatbot get advice that forgets what they said, agrees with them too easily and never tracks whether anything happened.
- **Their Language**: "I don't know where to start." "I keep going in circles." "I need someone to tell me if this is a bad idea." "What should I actually do this week?" "I have too many ideas and no plan."

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Gambit writes things down and pushes back. Every conversation updates one living record of the goal: plan, risks, decisions, people and progress. It uses proven planning methods (premortems, red-teaming, negotiation prep, forecasting) without making the user learn them. It's private by construction: no account, no server, and data stays on the device.
- **Competitors**: ChatGPT and Claude used as general chat (the real default). Coaching apps and AI life coaches. Goal trackers and planners (Notion templates, habit apps).
- **Market Position**: Niche. Free, open source, serious about thinking, modest in presentation.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Candid, practical, steady, curious, unfussy
- **Tone**: Casual but not chatty. Serious about the work, light in delivery.
- **Voice Admires**: Field Notes (the notebook brand), Basecamp and 37signals writing, a good editor's margin notes

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Write it down. Tell the truth kindly. Next step over big picture. Your goal, your data.
- **Mission**: Give anyone going after a hard goal the kind of clear, honest strategic thinking that usually only comes with a good mentor, and keep it on the page where they can use it.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Open-source reputation. People use it, share it and star it.
- **Key Metrics**: GitHub stars, first-session completion (key entered through first plan written), returning sessions per goal, organic mentions.

## Standing tension
Gambit needs the user's own model API key, and the chosen audience mostly doesn't have one. Key setup is the largest brand risk: it's the first thing a generalist meets, and right now it looks like a developer tool. Every brand decision should treat that screen as the front door.
